01

Services

Crafted Webs provides web design, logo creation, and photo editing services. We create websites that are visual in nature. Due to educational limitations, backend functionality (such as databases, user authentication, and server-side processing) is a service we are working toward and hope to offer in the near future once perfected.

All services are delivered with the utmost care and craftsmanship. If you have questions about what is or isn't included in your project scope, please reach out before booking.

03

Payment

Payments must be made upfront for smaller projects. For larger engagements, a 40% deposit is required before work begins, with the remaining balance due upon project completion.

Deposits are non-refundable once work has started. This protects both parties and ensures commitment to the agreed scope.

04

Refund Policy

Refunds are only applicable before work has begun. Once a project is in progress, partial refunds may be considered depending on the amount of work completed at the time of the request. No refunds are issued for completed work.

05

Revisions

Every project includes a minimum of 3 revisions at each stage of the design process. This ensures you have ample opportunity to refine and perfect the final result. Additional revision rounds beyond the included limit may be quoted separately.

06

Copyright & Ownership

Upon receipt of full payment, the client owns all rights to the final delivered design files and assets. Crafted Webs retains no claim over the completed work once payment is settled.

07

Website Publication

Crafted Webs is responsible for designing and building your website. If you wish to publish your site on Google (i.e. accessible via a custom domain), you are responsible for providing and managing the domain name.

If you wish for your site to be accessible via a shareable link without a custom domain, we can facilitate this as an additional service for an agreed-upon fee.

08

Project Timelines

Estimated completion times are provided for every project and discussed at the outset. Timelines may vary based on project complexity, the volume of revision requests, and the speed of client feedback and content delivery.

Timely feedback from the client is essential to keeping projects on schedule. Delays caused by unresponsiveness may extend the estimated delivery date.

09

Client Responsibilities

Clients must provide all necessary content, branding materials, images, and feedback in a timely manner. Delays in client communication or content delivery may result in extended project deadlines, for which Crafted Webs accepts no liability.

10

Rejection of Work

If a client is dissatisfied with the initial designs, they may request revisions within the agreed revision limit. If a project is rejected entirely after significant work has been completed, no refund will be issued for the work already performed.

11

Termination of Services

Crafted Webs reserves the right to terminate services under the following conditions: the client is unresponsive for more than 10 consecutive days, the client engages in unethical or abusive behavior, or the client violates any agreed-upon terms.

In such cases, no refund will be issued for work already completed.

12

Crafted Webs respects client privacy and will not share, sell, or distribute personal information to third parties. Completed project work may be featured in our portfolio unless a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is signed prior to project commencement.

13

Maintenance & Support

Website maintenance and ongoing updates are offered as a separate service agreement. Once a website is delivered, future edits, updates, or troubleshooting may incur additional fees unless a maintenance plan has been agreed upon in advance.

14

Late Fees

Payments are due according to the schedule agreed upon at the start of the project. If a payment is more than 7 days late, a 10% late fee may be applied to the outstanding balance.
